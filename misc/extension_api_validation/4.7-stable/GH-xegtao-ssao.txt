The screen-space ambient occlusion implementation of the Forward+ renderer was replaced with XeGTAO (Ground Truth Ambient Occlusion) with bent normals.
The SSAO settings were replaced accordingly: power, detail, horizon and sharpness were removed as they have no equivalent, thin_occluder_compensation was added,
and the quality setting now takes the number of denoising passes and whether bent normals are computed. The Very Low quality level was removed.
No compatibility methods are provided as the previous settings cannot be mapped to the new implementation.
